The Secret Resilience of Tropical Wetlands

Wetlands and freshwater swamps are frequently mischaracterized as inhospitable wastelands. In reality, these complex ecosystems function as vital biological buffers. Apex predators such as alligators play an unexpected role as ecosystem engineers. During dry seasons, alligators excavate deep depressions known as "alligator holes" to retain moisture. These depressions become critical aquatic sanctuaries for fish, amphibians, and wading birds when surrounding wetlands dry out completely. Consequently, the presence of alligators directly sustains the biodiversity and survival of numerous interrelated species.

  • Part 5 (Q26–35): Read a text and choose missing words
  • Part 6 (Gaps 1–6): Read and write one missing word in each gap
Writing Paper (3 Partes • 45 min):
  • Part 1: Sentence transformations (auto-checked)
