The Saturday Bike Club

by Clara Bennett, aged 15

Most of them only needed simple repairs, such as new inner tubes or oiled chains. 16 We asked the local community centre if we could borrow their courtyard every Saturday morning, and the manager immediately agreed.

Before our first session, we worried that nobody would turn up. On Saturday at nine o'clock, however, ten people were already waiting outside the gate with their bikes. 17 Fortunately, two retired mechanics who lived nearby offered to teach us how to adjust brakes and gears properly.

Over the next few months, the workshop grew busier and friendlier. Teenagers, parents, and elderly cyclists all worked side by side, sharing spanners and advice. 18 Seeing someone ride away smiling on a bike that had been forgotten for years is always the best moment of my week.

Keeping the club completely free has not always been easy because replacement cables and tyres can be expensive. 19 Thanks to their generosity, we now have a full cupboard of spare parts ready for anyone who needs them.

Next spring, our school wants to set up a second repair stand during lunchtime so students can cycle to class safely. 20 Even if I eventually move away for university, I know the project will keep rolling without me.
